

# Notion Inverted

*A little piece of Notion, reimagined for RemNote.*

A minimalist RemNote theme featuring a crisp white sidebar and warm beige document paper. Designed to bring the calm, spacious feeling of Notion into your everyday study workspace.

## ✧ Design philosophy

**Simple. Soft. Distraction-free.**

Notion Inverted takes the familiar Notion aesthetic and gives it a little twist: instead of a beige sidebar and white editor, it uses a clean white sidebar paired with warm beige document paper.

The result is a workspace that feels light, organised, and comfortable to study in.

## ✦ Features

- Clean white sidebar
- Warm beige document background
- Soft, understated dividers
- Muted secondary text
- Subtle hover effects
- Minimalist Notion-inspired aesthetic

## Installation

1. Open RemNote.
2. Navigate to the CSS snippets or themes section.
3. Find **Notion Inverted**.
4. Install and enable the theme.
